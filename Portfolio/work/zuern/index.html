Zürn Tafelwelt

A client website for Zürn Tafelwelt e.K., connecting systems for clinics, practices and professional working environments. From the first structure and concept to design, development and technical setup.

Visit website↗
video
01 / A closer lookRecorded from the working website

Räume, die mitarbeiten.

The company offers systems for organising professional spaces. The task was to make that offer easier to understand: clarify the content, connect the product areas and give different audiences a useful starting point.

Structure before detail.

I reorganised the information around the company's audiences and working environments, then developed the visual direction and responsive frontend. Client feedback shaped the content and interface through successive iterations.

04 / A clear next step. Navigation and page transitions.

Designed to move
between screens.

The visual hierarchy stays clear on a small screen. Navigation opens into a focused menu that fills the screen.

One connected digital presence.

A responsive website with a clear path from understanding the systems to starting an enquiry. My work included the information architecture, design, implementation and deployment setup.

Screens and recordings document the website during development. Product diagrams are explanatory illustrations, not photographs of individual products.
