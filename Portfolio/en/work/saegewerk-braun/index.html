Sägewerk Braun

The goal was to create a website that presents Sägewerk Braun, its work and services in a clear and authentic way. I took care of the entire process, from planning the content and visual design to photography and developing the finished website.

Visit website↗
video

First, getting to know the business.

To show what makes Sägewerk Braun special, I first wanted to understand the business and its work. Together, we planned the content and captured photographs and drone footage, which I then edited for the website. The imagery became an important part of its visual identity.

From the workshop to the web.

I designed and developed the website from the ground up, with a clear structure that helps visitors find what they're looking for. The goal was to bring content, photography and design together into an experience that feels consistent, intuitive and easy to navigate.

Built for everyday use.

The website was designed to look and feel good on any device. I focused on smooth interactions, fast loading times and reliable performance, even on slower internet connections. The result is a website that feels simple to use without compromising on the details.
