Sägewerk Braun

A website shaped from the business itself: customer-facing content, original photography and a clear view of the sawmill, timber shop and packaging services.

Content / Photography / Design / Development
Website / Photography / Content / 2026

Visit website↗
video
01 — A closer lookRecorded from the working website

Context

The material came first.

The business had more to show than a list of services. I helped create and organise the content needed to explain the work clearly, using product photography, drone imagery and image editing alongside the website design.

Approach

From workshop to website.

Raw business information became a structure organised around customers' questions. Original images show the site, products and materials; the copy explains the options and makes the next step towards an enquiry clear.

At any scale

A local business.
Easy to reach.

Services, practical information and direct contact, with a layout that adapts to the screen at hand.

Result

Content, images and interface together.

A customer-facing website that connects the company's services with the real place and work behind them. Photography is part of how the business is presented, not an afterthought.
