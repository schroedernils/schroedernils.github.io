Website hosting

The public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The provider is GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. Requests involve the processing of connection data needed to deliver the website, in particular IP address, time, requested resource and technical browser information.

GitHub states that it stores IP addresses for security purposes. The legal basis for hosting is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR: my legitimate interest in a secure and reliable portfolio. GitHub determines the exact retention period of hosting logs; I do not have a confirmed fixed period. Information about recipients, international processing and safeguards is provided in the GitHub privacy statement.

Local content and external links

Fonts, photographs and project videos are loaded from the same host as this website. There are no embedded YouTube videos, external fonts or contact forms. Preview videos load only when played. Links to GitHub or project websites open the external page only when you follow them; that provider’s privacy information applies there.

The website has no login, advertising profiles or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Contact by email

If you email me, I process your email address, the content and any other information you provide in order to respond. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for an enquiry relating to a contract, or otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR and my interest in responding.

Data is deleted once the enquiry has been fully handled and there are no legal retention obligations or legitimate reasons for further storage. Email is transmitted through your email provider and my email service; this website does not send messages itself.

Your cookie choice

The decision about statistics is stored under portfolio-consent in your browser’s Local Storage, including version, selection and expiry time. The choice expires after 183 days. This essential storage avoids asking again on every page change. The legal bases are § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Optional statistics are initially disabled. Rejecting does not affect your use of the website. You can change or withdraw your decision at any time using “Cookies” in the footer. After withdrawal, known Analytics cookies are deleted and the page is reloaded without Analytics code. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing that has already occurred.

Google Analytics 4 · optional

Google Analytics 4 is loaded only after your explicit consent. Without consent there is no connection to Google Analytics, including measurement requests without cookies. The provider for the EEA is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

The purpose is statistical analysis of how this portfolio is used. Collected data includes pages viewed, active visit time in a visible and focused browser window, navigation and clicks on contact links, scroll depth, viewed sections and photographs, and started project videos. This also includes technical device and browser information, language, approximate region and pseudonymous cookie identifiers. There is no session recording or tracking of text input. The IP address is processed for technical transmission and regional assignment; Google states that it discards EU IP addresses before logging them. The website does not send form content, names or email addresses as Analytics parameters. URL query parameters and fragments are removed from sent page addresses; referrals are limited to their original domain.

The cookies _ga and _ga_* distinguish visits. The website limits their lifetime to 183 days and does not automatically extend it on each visit. Google signals and enhanced automatic measurement are disabled in this property. Advertising and personalised advertising are not enabled by this integration. The legal bases are Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG.

Data may also be processed by Google LLC in the USA. For international transfers, Google describes the EU and US Data Privacy Framework and supplementary standard contractual clauses. Details: Google privacy policy and Transfer frameworks. Consent is voluntary and can be withdrawn at any time through cookie settings.

The retention period for user and event data is set to 14 months, without resetting on new activity. According to Google, changes to event retention take effect after 24 hours. This period does not automatically apply to all aggregated standard reports and is independent of the cookie lifetime, which is limited to six months. Google’s data processing terms were accepted in the account on 6 October 2026.

Your rights

Subject to the legal requirements, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability (Art. 15 to 20 GDPR). You may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). You may withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR). Contact me at the email address above.

You may also complain to a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg or the authority where you live or work.

Changes

This statement will be updated when technical services or purposes change. If purposes requiring consent change, a new selection will be requested.
