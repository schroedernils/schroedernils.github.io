Think it through.Make it work.

Nils Schröder (19)
Stuttgart, Germany

Computer Science
University of Stuttgart

I like turning ideas and complex problems into things that feel clear, useful and complete. I care about how they work and how they feel to use.

I learn by building, questioning my decisions and working out why something didn't work. Feedback becomes the next iteration, from the relationships between parts of a system to the smallest interaction.

From the first rough idea to a considered result, I bring together technical thinking, structure, visual detail and photography.
